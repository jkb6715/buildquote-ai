How BuildQuote AI will work

  1. Describe the job

    Type or paste your notes in plain language, the way you would explain the job to a coworker.

  2. Add measurements and details

    Enter dimensions, materials, and anything the customer asked for.

  3. Get an AI-written draft

    The software organizes everything into a scope of work, a material checklist, and a proposal.

  4. Review, edit, and send

    You stay in control. Adjust the wording and numbers, then share the proposal with your customer.
